# Commit Best Practices

**Always commit when you finish your work.** This ensures:
- Your work is saved and versioned
- You can track your progress
- Collaboration is possible
- Rollbacks are available if needed

**If you are on a branch for an individual feature, also push.** This ensures:
- Your work is backed up remotely
- Others can review your work
- CI/CD pipelines can run
- Your work is discoverable

**When to commit:**
- After completing a task or bug fix
- Before switching to a different task
- At the end of a work session
- When you reach a logical stopping point

**When to push (in addition to commit):**
- When working on a feature branch
- When you want to create a PR/MR
- When you want to trigger CI/CD
- When you want others to see your work
- Before ending your session on a feature branch

**Good commit messages include:**
- What was changed
- Why it was changed
- Any known issues or next steps
